Fig. S3 PaySim PTHW reliability diagrams. Rows are folds and columns are raw, Platt and isotonic Test probabilities; markers and line styles identify five seeds. Points show mean predicted probability and observed fraud fraction in nonempty bins of the fixed ten-bin uniform partition. Lines are visual guides and can span empty bins; the diagonal denotes agreement. Of 450 bins across 45 series, 299 are nonempty and 50 nonempty bins have fewer than ten observations. Bins are not pooled. Sparse points can be unstable, and these diagrams do not demonstrate improved ranking or decisions. Soft halos are decorative fixed-display-width strokes along the original paths and do not represent uncertainty intervals. Single-point series receive marker halos only.
