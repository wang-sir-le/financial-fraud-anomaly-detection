Fig. S9 Direct merchant input and accessible history in six fixed retrospective BankSim conditions. (a) Whole-window tiles report TP, recall and uncaptured frauds (P-TP) at K=5,950 among P=2,160 frauds. Row colors identify direct merchant input. (b) Bars show I=100J/P in percentage points for the whole window and three fixed segments; labels give I, J and P. J=(TP[Horig]-TP[H7])M-on-(TP[Horig]-TP[H7])M-off. M-off removes only the direct merchant predictor; merchant remains the history key. H7 uses seven strictly earlier complete simulation steps. Horig retains the original 1/7/30-step aggregates and strictly earlier recency/availability, whose source may extend beyond 30 steps. Segment budgets are local, so their selections do not decompose the whole-window list. These single fits under C07 and seed 42 use the already-exposed development environment, without confidence intervals; they do not establish substitution, sufficiency or equivalence
