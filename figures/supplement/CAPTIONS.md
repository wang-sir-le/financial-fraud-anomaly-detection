# Final supplement figure captions

Fig. S1 Joint changes in AP and recall under the dataset-specific protocols. This alternative view uses the same means as preserved Fig. S8; it introduces no new evidence. PaySim AP is based on clipped-logit Platt scores and recall on transferred thresholds; IEEE-CIS uses raw scores and fixed window capacity

Fig. S2 Alternative display of recall differences for IEEE-CIS C02 at the primary 3% capacity. Squares and horizontal bars show the frozen estimates and pointwise 95% intervals from main Table 9 and Fig. 5a–c, expressed in percentage points. Intervals wholly below and above zero indicate negative and positive differences, respectively; an interval crossing zero does not establish equivalence. This explanatory view adds no independent evidence and does not establish a temporal trend or a causal mechanism

Fig. S3 PaySim PTHW reliability diagrams. Rows are folds and columns are raw, Platt and isotonic Test probabilities; markers and line styles identify five seeds. Points show mean predicted probability and observed fraud fraction in nonempty bins of the fixed ten-bin uniform partition. Lines are visual guides and can span empty bins; the diagonal denotes agreement. Of 450 bins across 45 series, 299 are nonempty and 50 nonempty bins have fewer than ten observations. Bins are not pooled. Sparse points can be unstable, and these diagrams do not demonstrate improved ranking or decisions. Soft halos are decorative and do not represent uncertainty intervals

Fig. S4 PaySim AP contrasts before and after score processing. Open circles show five-seed paired mean differences from raw scores; filled squares show clipped/Platt differences for PH−P0, PTH−PT and PTH−P0 in all three test windows. Clipped and Platt endpoints coincide for each displayed contrast. Connectors link score stages; they represent neither uncertainty intervals nor temporal trajectories. No intervals are depicted. Paired intervals for core effects and processing changes remain in Tables S18–S19; the other evaluated contrasts remain in those tables

Fig. S5 Core component differences and pointwise conditional 95% intervals. AP is on its original scale and recall is in percentage points at an exact 3% test-window budget. PaySim displays raw, clipped and saved-Platt stages; IEEE-CIS uses raw scores. Clipped and Platt intervals coincide. Windows are shown separately; these intervals do not test cross-window differences

Fig. S6 Empirical cumulative distribution of the existing PaySim Fold 3 weighting-effect draws. Dashed lines mark the observed effect and original percentile endpoints. Primary L=7 covers the fixed 345-step window. This figure is a post-hoc view of retained draws, not new uncertainty analysis

Fig. S7 Descriptive BankSim transaction counts, fraud counts and step contributions to whole-window H0/H1 at 3%. (a) Counts; (b) H0=B0H-B0 and (c) H1=B1H-B1, shown as five-seed means for both models. Steps are simulation units, not independent business days. Lines describe the realised sequence and do not establish stationarity, a dependence horizon or interval coverage

Fig. S8 Preserved original descriptive component-direction figure (former main Fig. 5). PaySim History is the time-context plus recipient-history bundle, not PH. Recall uses transferred thresholds for PaySim and exact 3% capacity for IEEE-CIS; units are percentage points. AP uses Platt and raw scores, respectively. No intervals are shown and the panels have separate symmetric scales

Fig. S9 Direct merchant input and accessible history in six fixed retrospective BankSim conditions. (a) Whole-window tiles report TP, recall and uncaptured frauds (P-TP) at K=5,950 among P=2,160 frauds. Row colors identify direct merchant input. (b) Bars show I=100J/P in percentage points for the whole window and three fixed segments; labels give I, J and P. J=(TP[Horig]-TP[H7])M-on-(TP[Horig]-TP[H7])M-off. M-off removes only the direct merchant predictor; merchant remains the history key. H7 uses seven strictly earlier complete simulation steps. Horig retains the original 1/7/30-step aggregates and strictly earlier recency/availability, whose source may extend beyond 30 steps. Segment budgets are local, so their selections do not decompose the whole-window list. These single fits under C07 and seed 42 use the already-exposed development environment, without confidence intervals; they do not establish substitution, sufficiency or equivalence
