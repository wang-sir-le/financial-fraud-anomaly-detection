Fig. S7 Descriptive BankSim temporal structure and step contributions to whole-window capture effects at 3% capacity. (a) Transaction counts (left axis, blue) and fraud counts (right axis, orange dashed line) for all 54 simulation steps, 126-179. Each step contains 40 frauds. (b) H0=B0H-B0 and (c) H1=B1H-B1, shown for LightGBM and XGBoost as five-seed means of paired captured-fraud count differences. Each condition and seed uses its original completed-Test-window top-3% selection (K=5,950); captured frauds are attributed to their simulation steps before differences and seed means are formed. No per-step selection, capacity allocation or reranking is performed. Step contributions sum to the original whole-window H0/H1 estimates. Lines are visual guides to the realised sequence; the plot adds no uncertainty intervals and does not establish stationarity or a temporal mechanism. Simulation steps are not independent business days.
