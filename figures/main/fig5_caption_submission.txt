Fig. 5 IEEE-CIS combined and component effects across three temporal Test windows at the exact 3% completed-window review budget using raw scores. Columns correspond to Folds 1–3. (a–c) LightGBM combined effects, M2−M0: captured-fraud difference (ΔTP), recall difference (percentage points), and average-precision difference (0–1 scale), with their original pointwise 95% intervals from Table 9. The horizontal point–interval plots display ΔTP only and share the same −40 to +40 range. (d–f) History increments (H, M1−M0) and incremental weighting above history (W, M2−M1) from Table 10. Numerical columns and heatmaps show ΔTP point estimates for LightGBM, Logistic and directly paired Logistic-minus-LightGBM effects. All three heatmaps share a symmetric zero-centred ±120 ΔTP scale. The two intervals printed beneath each component table belong to the direct workflow effect differences; Table 10 and the data export report the full within-workflow intervals. LightGBM averages five paired seed effects; Logistic uses one fixed fit per condition. Intervals are the original pointwise conditional 95% percentile intervals from 5,000 paired moving-block draws per window, without multiple-comparison adjustment, conditional on fixed workflows and observed windows. Colours encode signed point estimates, not significance. Preprocessing differs between workflows. Sequential component point estimates sum to the combined point estimate; interval endpoints are not added or subtracted
