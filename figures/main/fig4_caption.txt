Fig. 4 BankSim reference-dependent capture and post-hoc capture exchanges at the exact 3% completed-window budget (K=5,950). H0=B0H−B0; H1=B1H−B1; D=H1−H0. (a–c) Post-hoc descriptive five-seed paired means of newly captured frauds G, lost frauds L (non-negative), and net change ΔTP=G−L. Colours encode magnitudes, not statistical significance; G/L have no separate uncertainty intervals. (d) Paired effects from the original BankSim validation, with moving-block intervals at a block length of seven simulation steps: H0 uses 95% pointwise intervals; H1 and D use 98.75% Bonferroni marginal intervals for four primary comparisons. (e) The same H1 effects and intervals on a magnified scale. Intervals condition on fixed models and the observed window. Footer values are observed-point arithmetic bounds, not confidence limits
