Fig. 6 Capacity, cost sensitivity and reference effects across three temporal Test windows. (a) Recall differences at c=100: PaySim PTHW−P0 under Validation alert caps and IEEE-CIS LightGBM M2−M0 under exact completed-Test-window budgets using raw scores; vertical scales differ. (b) PaySim percentage cost reduction at a 3% Validation cap, averaged as seedwise 100(C_P0−C_PTHW)/C_P0 with C=cFN+FP; the cost axis is logarithmic. (c) PaySim PTHW recall at the saved exact 0.1%, 1%, 3% and 5% Test budgets, ranking each seed’s Platt scores separately with K=ceil(qN). This descriptive readout uses completed-window ranking. (d) Realized PaySim Test alert rates, varying the Validation cap at c=100 (left) or c at a 3% Validation cap (right); solid lines and filled markers denote PTHW, and dashed lines and open markers denote P0. Validation caps do not impose exact Test workloads. Panels (a–d) show five-seed means without uncertainty intervals; connecting lines guide reading between evaluated scenarios. Curve glow is decorative. (e) Previously reported reference recall effects and their saved pointwise conditional 95% percentile intervals from 5,000 paired moving-block draws per window. PaySim uses the 3% Validation cap and c=100; IEEE-CIS uses the exact 3% Test budget. The groups use separate horizontal scales. Intervals condition on fixed fitted workflows and observed windows
