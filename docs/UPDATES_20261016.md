## Evidence retained

Figure 5 retains all 81 numerical values at the reported precision of Tables 9 and 10. Colours encode direction rather than significance. Figure 6 retains 111 plotted points and six existing intervals from saved aggregate inputs. Figure S2 retains the three original effects and intervals, including the interval crossing zero. Figure S3 retains all 45 series and 299 nonempty-bin points; its halos are decorative rather than confidence bands. Figure S9 retains the six conditions and four contrasts from S22. Its local segment budgets do not decompose the whole-window selection, and the retrospective single-seed results do not establish substitution, sufficiency or equivalence.

No model fitting, transaction-level scoring, calibration, ranking or Bootstrap sampling was performed for this publication. Original experimental archives and statistical estimands remain unchanged. Figure reproduction checks cover saved aggregates and rendering, with possible font/renderer differences; they do not establish independent raw-to-model reproduction.
